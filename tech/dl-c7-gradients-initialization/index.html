3.1.现象与动机

​ 符号 设第$k$个隐藏层的权重为 $\beta_k,\Omega_k$ ,激活函数为 $a[x]$ ,上一层的输入为 $h_k$ ,仿射变换的结果为 $f_k$ ,每一层会进行如下运算:

$$\begin{aligned} \bold f_k &= \boldsymbol \beta_k + \bold \Omega_k \bold h_k\\ &= \boldsymbol \beta_k+\bold \Omega_ka[\bold f_{k-1}] \end{aligned}$$

​ 如果将偏置$\boldsymbol \beta_k$初始化为$\bold 0$, $\bold \Omega_k$每一个值初始化为$N(0,\sigma^2)$. 当$\sigma$很小或很大时, 深层网络重复的仿射变换可能会让输出值超出浮点数表示范围.

3.2.He初始值的推导

​ 符号 设上一层和当前层Affine映射的结果为$\bold f, \bold f'$, 维度分别为$D_h,D_{h'}$:

$$\bold h=a[\bold f]\\ \bold f' = \boldsymbol \beta+\bold \Omega \bold h$$

​ $f_i, \beta_i, \Omega_{ij}$是属于各自矩阵的标量, 其中$\Omega_{ij}$使用均值为0的正态分布初始化, $\beta_i$直接初始化为0, 另外两个的$\sigma$分别为$\sigma_f^2, \sigma_\Omega^2$

​ 则, 对于当前层Affine映射结果的每一个标量$f_i'$, 有:

$$\begin{aligned} E[f_i'] &= E \left[\beta_i+\sum_{j=1}^{D_h}\Omega_{ij}h_j \right]\\ &= E[\beta_i]+\sum_{j=1}^{D_h} E[\Omega_{ij}h_j]\\ &= E[\beta_i]+\sum_{j=1}^{D_h} E[\Omega_{ij}]E[h_j]\\ &= 0+\sum_{j=1}^{D_h} 0·E[h_j] = 0 \end{aligned}$$

​ 所以, 有:

$$\begin{aligned} \sigma_{f'_i}^2 &= E[f_i'^2]-E[f_i']^2 \\ &= E\left[\left(\beta_i+\sum_{j=1}^{D_h}\Omega_{ij}h_j \right)^2 \right] - 0\\ &= E\left[\left(\sum_{j=1}^{D_h}\Omega_{ij}h_j \right)^2 \right]\\ &= \sum_{j=1}^{D_h}E[\Omega_{ij}^2]E[h_j^2] &(独立性)\\ &=\sum_{j=1}^{D_h}\sigma_\Omega^2E[h_j^2]=\sigma_\Omega^2\sum_{j=1}^{D_h}E[h_j^2] \end{aligned}$$

​ 又因为$\bold h = a[\bold f]$, 有如下公式(证明见Problem7.14):

$$E[h] = \sigma_f^2/2$$

​ 则有:

$$\sigma_{f_i'}^2 =\frac{D_h}{2} \sigma_\Omega^2 \sigma_f^2$$

​ 要使这一层Affine映射的结果的方差与上一层相同, 即$\sigma_{f'}^2 = \sigma_f^2$ 可以得出:

$$\sigma_\Omega^2 = \frac2{D_h}$$

§Problems

Problem7.14

​ Consider a random variable $a$ with variance $Var[a] = σ^2$ and a symmetrical distribution around the mean $E[a] = 0$. Prove that if we pass this variable through the ReLU function:

$$b = ReLU[a] = \left\{ \begin{aligned} 0 &, a<0\\ a &, a\geq 0 \end{aligned} \right.$$

​ then the second moment of the transformed variable is $E[b^2]=\sigma/2$.

​ proof 先求分布函数?

$$F_b(x)=P(b<x)=P(ReLU[a]<x)= \left\{ \begin{aligned} &0&, x<0\\ &F_a(x)&,x \geq 0 \end{aligned} \right. \\ f_b(x) = \left\{ \begin{aligned} &0&,x<0\\ &f_a(x)&,x\geq0 \end{aligned} \right.$$

​ 则有:

$$\begin{aligned} E[b^2] &= \int_{-\infin}^{+\infin} x^2 f_b(x)dx \\ &= \int_0^{+\infin} x^2f_a(x)dx \end{aligned}$$

​ 又, 根据题目, 有如下条件:

$$\int _{-\infin}^{+\infin}xf_a(x)dx=0 \\ \int _{-\infin}^{+\infin}x^2f_a(x)dx=\sigma^2 \\ f_a(x)=f_a(-x)$$

​ 则根据$x^2f_a(x)$必为偶函数, 有:

$$\int_0^{+\infin} x^2f_a(x)dx=\frac{\sigma^2}{2}$$

​ $\blacksquare$
