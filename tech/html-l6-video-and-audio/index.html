L6.Video and audio

发布于 2026年1月31日 | 分类: HTML
← 上一篇L5.Images6 / 8下一篇 →L7.table

1.video

<video src="rabbit320.webm" controls>
  <p>
    Your browser doesn't support HTML video. Here is a
    <a href="rabbit320.webm">link to the video</a> instead.
  </p>
</video>

src类似于img中的src

controls表示用户可以控制视频播放, 而控制的interface由浏览器默认, 或者自己通过js构建

包含在video element中的<p>是fallback content, 和img中的alt一样, 在视频无法播放时会显示这段文字.

2.video format

常见的视频格式诸如OGG, WAV, MP4 and WebM被称为container formats, 一个container formats通常包含了metadata, audio tracks, video tracks, text tracks.

audio tracks会使用audio codec压缩audio, 同理video tracks.

e.g.

  1. A WebM container typically packages Vorbis or Opus audio with VP8/VP9 video. This is supported in all modern browsers, though older versions may not work.
  2. An MP4 container often packages AAC or MP3 audio with H.264 video. This is also supported in all modern browsers.
  3. The Ogg container tends to use Vorbis audio and Theora video. This is best supported in Firefox and Chrome, but has basically been superseded by the better quality WebM format.

同时包含多个格式可以提高在不同设备上成功播放视频的概率:

<video controls>
  <source src="rabbit320.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
  <source src="rabbit320.webm" type="video/webm" />
  <p>
    Your browser doesn't support this video. Here is a
    <a href="rabbit320.mp4">link to the video</a> instead.
  </p>
</video>

上面我们把<source>分离出来, 变成<video>内的一个元素, source中的type不是必须的, 但有了type可以帮助浏览器直接下载可以播放的格式, 而不是一个一个下载然后尝试.

3.other video features

<video
  controls
  width="400"
  height="400"
  autoplay
  loop
  muted
  preload="auto"
  poster="poster.png">
  <source src="rabbit320.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
  <source src="rabbit320.webm" type="video/webm" />
  <p>
    Your browser doesn't support this video. Here is a
    <a href="rabbit320.mp4">link to the video</a> instead.
  </p>
</video>

其中, poster属性是视频的封面.

preload控制缓存方式:

  • "none" does not buffer the file
  • "auto" buffers the media file
  • "metadata" buffers only the metadata for the file

4.audio

<audio controls>
  <source src="viper.mp3" type="audio/mp3" />
  <source src="viper.ogg" type="audio/ogg" />
  <p>
    Your browser doesn't support this audio file. Here is a
    <a href="viper.mp3">link to the audio</a> instead.
  </p>
</audio>

audio element和video几乎一样, 除了不支持width and height and poster

5.video text tracks

WebVTT文件使用text string记录了视频字幕, 这些字符串通常被称为cues.

Cues有不同的类型, 包括了: subtitles(外文翻译), captions, timed descriptions

WEBVTT

1
00:00:22.230 --> 00:00:24.606
This is the first subtitle.

2
00:00:30.739 --> 00:00:34.074
This is the second.

…

通过track元素调用:

<video controls>
  <source src="example.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
  <source src="example.webm" type="video/webm" />
  <track kind="subtitles" src="subtitles_es.vtt" srclang="es" label="Spanish" />
</video>
